FAQ

Questions people ask.

Do I need an eSIM phone?

No. Any phone with a SIM slot works, as long as it is network-unlocked.

What do I get for €9.99?

A SUPERSIM SIM card. Data packages are bought separately.

How do packages activate?

Automatically on your SIM, right after purchase. You do not scan a code or install an app.

Can I add more packages later?

Yes. Each new package activates on the same SIM.

How can I pay?

By card through Keepz, for customers outside Georgia.
